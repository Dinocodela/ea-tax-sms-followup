Simulate a Mixmax email

Creates a local test event. No external API is called in demo mode. Use an address containing “multi” to get a contact with three numbers.

How this works

This app follows a Mixmax email with a scheduled RingCentral text. It does not text at the moment the email is sent.

  1. An email goes out from a Mixmax sequence that has texts set up under Sequences.
  2. The app polls Mixmax every couple of minutes (or click Poll now).
  3. It matches the recipient’s email to one RingCentral contact and picks their text number. If they have several numbers, it waits for you to choose one under Recipients.
  4. At the due time it checks again for replies, stop conditions, opt-outs and the sequence status.
  5. In test mode it only simulates the text. Live sending also needs an allowlisted number and your approval.

Status guide

To review: a draft waiting for someone to check and approve it; it expires if not reviewed within 24 hours of its due time. Scheduled: waiting for its due time. Needs number: the recipient has several numbers; choose one. Simulated: test mode, nothing was sent. Sent: RingCentral accepted it (delivery not confirmed). Skipped: the app could not safely create a send. Cancelled: a stop condition or manual action prevented sending. Held: too old, needs review. Unknown: the send result could not be confirmed; check RingCentral before retrying.

Any text a recipient sends back (including STOP) blocks future texts to that number. Clear one only after reading the conversation and confirming follow-up is appropriate. Cancelled texts stay cancelled.
